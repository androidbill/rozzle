    How To Play Rozzle

    Rozzle is a 2-4 player "push-your-luck" dice game where players aim to be the first to reach 10,000 points. Players roll six dice, setting aside scoring combinations (1s, 5s, or sets), and can either bank points or risk them by re-rolling remaining dice, aiming for 10,000+ points.

    Basic Scoring Rules

    • Single 1: 100 points
    • Single 5: 50 points
    • Three 1s: 1,000 points
    • Three 2s: 200 points
    • Three 3s: 300 points
    • Three 4s: 400 points
    • Three 5s: 500 points
    • Three 6s: 600 points
    • Four, Five, or Six of a Kind: Doubles the 3-of-a-kind score for each additional die (e.g., four 2s = 400, five 2s = 800).
    • Straight (1-2-3-4-5-6): 1,500 points.
    • Three Pairs: 1,500 points.
    • Four of a Kind + Pair: 1,500 points.
    • Two Triplets (e.g., 3-3-3 and 4-4-4): 2,500 points.

    Key Game Terms

    • Rozzle: If no scoring dice appear in a roll, it is a "Farkle," and you lose all points accumulated in that turn.
    • Hot Dice: If all six dice are used for scoring in one turn, the player can pick up all six and roll again to continue increasing their score.
    • Opening Score: Rules often require a minimum score (often 500 or 1000) to start banking points.
    • Winning: The first player to reach or exceed 10,000 points on their turn wins, though some rules allow other players one final turn.
